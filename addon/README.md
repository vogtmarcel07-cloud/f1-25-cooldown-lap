# Cool-down Lap

After the chequered flag the player keeps the car and drives a cool-down lap into parc ferme. The AI top 3 park in
parc ferme too, with 1/2/3 boards in front of the cars.

## Options
- Cool-down lap on/off, at most N seconds, ends at parc ferme / pit lane / after the time.
- AI top 3 park in parc ferme; parc ferme marking (prototype).

**Requires:** F1 25 (Steam), Swofty Launcher 1.6.10 or newer. Works with the launcher's own bindings, no test bindings needed. Tested in Grand Prix mode.
